## Suggested fixes (BLOCKER and MAJOR; not applied)
- A-1: R68: delayed effects come first at start of turn and after the trap window at end of turn (R62); several delayed effects resolve in creation order; GY-zone triggers resolve after hand triggers, in GY order; only spells flagged `returnToHandAtEndOfTurn` when played that turn return. Align §5.1 and §6.2.
- A-2: R73: either transfer ownership of swapped library cards to the new library's player, or state that drawn swapped cards go to the drawer's hand under the drawer's control; add the chosen exception to R12 and §3.2.
- A-3: §4.5/R59: "Each forced attack is its own combat: the state check runs after it, and the next forced attacker attacks only if the target is still on the field."
- A-4: §4.4 and a new R-row: "A hit whose amount is 0 before step 1 is not a damage instance: Divine Shield stays and nothing triggers (Hearthstone)."
- A-5: Fuse R-row: put fused stats in one place (transient def's printed stats or `buffs`, not both, and delete the other sentence); transient def radiant form = base form (or fuse both forms); the result keeps the target's zone, position, damage, exertion, summonedTurn and radiant flag; define trap and Field Spell fusion.
- A-6: Extend R65: outside play an embiggen card's cost is its base price; an X-cost card counts as 0 in every cost comparison except where exempt (#94); `costOverride` never applies to X-cost cards. Cite in #7, #30, #51, R66.
- A-7: R43: "every Heroic Power in any zone rolls at start of game" or "Quickdraw cards cannot be returned by mulligan".
- A-8: Add `statsOverride` to R57/cloneInstance and #22's memory (`{defId, radiant, statsOverride}`); give Bread Token printed stats 0/0 in §7.
- A-9: R60: limit "N different cards" to picks among existing cards; catalog generation draws with repetition unless the card says "different" or it is a Discover. #95: "Field Spells, Traps and Field Traps; traps enter face-down".
- A-10: R-row: leaving the field resets damage, buffs, granted keywords, Vanilla, counters, memory, exertion and position; `costMod` and `costOverride` persist in every zone; the radiant flag persists; Reborn returns a reset instance.
- A-11: §2.5 rows for turn-clock expiry (e.g. the turn ends and open prompts are answered by the §10.7 AI) and the hard ceiling (e.g. a draw); name the durations as config values; define the `timeout` action in §10.2.
