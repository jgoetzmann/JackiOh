* Systems  
  * Jackioh is first and foremost a fusion of Heartstone combat and resource with Yugioh lanes  
  * Players gain 1 mana each turn up to 4\. It refreshes each turn. Turn 1 they have 1 mana. Turn 2 they have 2, etc.  
  * Players draw 1 card for free at the start of their turn  
  * There is a 30 turn cap, where an auto-draw happens  
  * If you have no valid actions, your turn is automatically skipped  
  * You can offer draw at any time (with appropriate spam restrictions)  
  * Player 1 draws 3 cards at the start of the game. Player 2 draws 4 cards. Etc. etc.  
  * Mulligan  
    * You predraw your opening hand before the game starts. You may choose to return any number of them to the deck to redraw a card in its place (without replacement).  
* Deckbuilding  
  * Decks consist of 20 cards without duplicates  
* Combat  
  * Units have attack and health values  
  * Units are permanent creatures. They are played into minion lanes.  
  * Units can target Units units to attack. When in combat, they deal damage to each other equal to one another’s attack. This damage persists between turns. When units reach 0 health, Destroy them and they are sent to the Graveyard  
  * Units can also attack Hero. Each player has 30 health, and loses if it reaches 0  
    * Unlike Units, players can heal above their starting health  
  * Units can attack once per turn or switch positions. There is Defense and Attack Position.  
  * Units are always summoned in Attack Position  
  * Units can’t attack on the turn they are summoned. They can, however, be switched to Defense Position  
* Zones  
  * Each player has a hand, library (deck), graveyard (GY), and exile  
  * Each player has 5 Unit Zones  
  * Each player has 5 Backrow Zones  
    * These hold Field Spells and Traps  
  * Lanes are a set of Backrow Zones and Unit Zones that both you and your opponent own  
    * A standard game has 5 Lanes  
* Notes  
  * A discover card OR a random generation card cannot discover nor randomly generate a copy of itself \[unless specifically specified it can\]  
* Keywords  
  * Card Type  
    * Unit, Spell, Field Spell, Trap, Field Trap  
  * Classifiers/tags  
    * Classifiers are tags attached to cards that certain cards might have special interactions with  
      * Rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic)  
      * Tribes (Human, Felinor, ..)  
      * Sets (Classic, Core, Boss, Boss-X)  
      * Tokens (see below)  
  * Tokens  
    * A card with the blanket Token tag cannot be generated UNLESS its specifically mentioned by name by another card. Most tokens are denoted with \#X.X&nbsp;  
    * Rush Token  
      * (1) cost 3/3 **Unit** with **Rush**  
    * Sheep Token  
      * (1) cost 1/1 **Unit** that is worth **2 Tributes**  
    * Felinor Token  
      * (1) cost 1/1 **Unit** with the **Felinor** tag.  
  * Piles  
    * Deck, GY, Exile  
  * Units  
    * Units on the Field that do combat  
  * Spell  
    * Single use card that is sent to the GY after its effects resolve  
  * Field Spell  
    * Persistent Spell that has lasting game effects  
    * Played in the Backrow  
  * Backrow  
    * Field Spells and Traps  
  * (Field )Trap  
    * Activates as soon as its activate condition is fulfilled regardless of the player turn.  
    * Send to the GY after activating unless a Field Trap  
    * Hidden to opponents, visible to you  
    * Played in the Backrow  
  * Taunt  
    * Keyword  
    * Enemy units must attack your units with Taunt before attacking units without or your Hero  
    * Units in Defense Position have Taunt  
  * Armor X  
    * Keyword is Armor 1  
    * Reduce damage taken by X  
    * Units in Defense Position have Armor \+1  
  * Heal  
    * Remove damage from a Unit up to its maximum health, or have a Hero gain that much life (no maximum cap)&nbsp;  
  * Rush  
    * Keyword  
    * Units can attack other Units the turn they are summoned but NOT Heroes  
  * Charge  
    * Keyword  
    * Units can attack other Units AND Hero the turn they are summoned  
  * First Strike  
    * Keyword  
    * Units with First Strike deal damage before Units without  
    * If the opposing Unit dies from this Unit’s damage, don’t take any damage at all  
  * Poisonous  
    * Keyword  
    * If this does 1 or more damage to a Unit, Destroy it  
  * Discover  
    * Choose from 1 of X options. X is 3 unless otherwise specified.  
  * Lifesteal  
    * Keyword  
    * Damage this deals is healed  
  * Reborn  
    * Keyword  
    * A unit with this on first death will respawn with (1) hp and without reborn  
  * Divine Shield  
    * Keyword  
    * Negate the first instance of damage this Unit takes. It then loses Divine Shield.  
  * Stack  
    * Keyword  
    * Can be played on top of another card on the Field in an appropriate zone. When the card with Stack leaves the field, the card under it is still present.  
  * Plague Token  
    * Static token placed on Permanents that is a resource other cards used  
    * Can put any number on a Permanent without limit&nbsp;  
    * Can only exist on the Field. Plague tokens are reset when the card leaves the Field.  
  * Cry  
    * When a card enters the Field for the first time, do this effect  
  * Death  
    * When a card is sent to the GY from Field, do this effect  
  * Aura  
    * Has an affect while in play  
  * Flicker  
    * The card leaves the Field then re-enters the Field  
  * Destroy  
    * Send a card from the Field to the GY  
  * Recruit  
    * Summon a card directly from your Library. Can be subject to constraints  
    * Summon works by looking from the top of the Library to the bottom  
  * Exile  
    * A very hard to interact with zone analogous to the GY. Exiled cards are sent here as well instead of to the GY.  
  * Trample  
    * Keyword  
    * Excess damage over the target Unit’s maximum health is sent to its owner’s Hero  
  * Discard  
    * Send a card from the hand directly to the GY  
  * Counter  
    * Send a summoned card to the GY without letting any of its effects resolve (it never enters the Field, it’s as it was never played, etc.)  
  * Permanent  
    * Unit, Field Spell, Trap  
  * Indestructible  
    * Keyword  
    * Can’t be Destroyed or take damage. Can be Exiled.  
    * When Destroyed, switches to attack position and loses Taunt for the turn.  
  * Immutable  
    * Keyword  
    * Can’t have its text changed / transformed  
  * Transform  
  * Summon  
    * Put on to the Field from a place that isn’t the Field  
  * Radiant  
    * Radiant cards are upgraded versions of normal cards. They cost the same, has the same art etc. They are separated under the cards with “\~\~\~  
  * Cleave  
    * Keyword  
    * Also damages Units in adjacent lanes  
  * Bounce  
    * Return card to owner’s hand  
  * Mana  
    * How much Mana you currently have  
    * Maximum Mana is how much mana you refresh with each turn. Grows \+1 per turn up to 4\. Can be exceeded with certain cards.  
  * Lock  
    * Cards can’t be Summoned to this Unit or Backrow Zone  
    * Existing cards in the Zone aren’t affected  
  * Tribute X  
    * Keyword is Tribute 1  
    * An additional cost of Sacrificing X of your Units from the Field to the GY in order to play this card / activate this effect  
    * Can Tribute things other than Units if specified  
  * Sacrifice  
    * Send a card you own on the Field to the GY. Bypasses Indestructible.  
  * Lucky X  
    * Keyword is Lucky 1  
    * Does a luck based action (coin flip, die, damage roll) an additional X times and takes the best outcome  
  * Cast on draw  
    * Plays the card instantly on draw, draw again (ie if I draw 1 and the card I draw is cast on draw it would cast and I’d draw again)  
  * Targets chosen randomly  
    * Plays the card and randomly choose a target  
  * Quickdraw  
    * Start of Game: add this to your starting hand. It replaces one of your initial draws.&nbsp;  
  * Start of turn  
    * Effect that triggers at the start of turn (before the draw)  
  * End of turn  
    * Effect that triggers at the end of turn  
  * Combo X  
    * Gain some additional effect if played after playing X cards in a given turn. \[if X is not specified it defaults to just after one card is played\]  
  * Echo X  
    * Keyword is Echo 1  
    * Recasts this card X times \[if X is not specified recast once\]  
  * Vanilla  
    * As a noun: **Unit** without text/other effects  
    * As a verb: Remove the text from a **Unit**  
  * Embiggen (X embiggen Y)  
    * On play it choose to pay X or Y mana, if you pay Y mana gain the embiggen effects  
  * Fatigue  
    * Happens when your Library is empty and you try to draw a card  
  * Fuse  
    * Combine the effects, stats of cards, and cost of cards (up to 4 mana)

    * Take control of
